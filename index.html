Questions owners ask

How much does a website cost for a service business?

Freelancers typically charge $1,500–$8,000 and agencies $5,000–$15,000+. Our standard 3–5 page rate is $1,995. Founding-cohort launch pricing is $750.

Do I pay anything upfront?

No. You get a flat written quote and pay only when the site is live and you like it.

Who owns the website?

You do. Domain, content and files are yours. No long contracts.

What if something breaks after launch?

2 revision rounds and 30 days of free fixes are included. After that, optional care plans start at $75/month.

Why not just use Wix or Squarespace?

You can. They run about $17–49/month plus 10–40 hours of your own time. We do the work so you can stay on jobs.

Where do you work?

Anywhere. We work remotely with service businesses worldwide.
