Guides

How to get more Google reviews (and do it within the rules)
  1. Ask every happy customer within 24 hours, in person and then by text with a direct review link.
  2. Make it one tap: use your profile's "Ask for reviews" link or a QR code.
  3. Never pay for reviews or offer anything in return. Google's policies prohibit incentivized reviews.
  4. Reply to every review, good or bad, within a day or two, using the customer's name and specifics.
Google Business Profile in 15 minutes
  1. Pick the most specific primary category, and add secondary ones.
  2. List every service, with a short description of each.
  3. Set hours, including holiday hours, and your service area.
  4. Add at least 10 real photos: team, vans, finished jobs.
  5. Post once a week: an offer, a recent job, or a tip.
The 5-minute missed-call fix
  1. Turn on a text-back message for missed calls in your phone app or system.
  2. Put "Call or text" next to your number on your site and profile.
  3. Add a simple online form that emails you and texts you.
  4. Check your voicemail greeting: say when you'll call back.
Before you sign with any agency: 7 questions
  1. What is the exact monthly price, and what's the setup fee?
  2. How long is the contract?
  3. Who owns the website, domain, Google profile and ad accounts?
  4. What do I get in each monthly report?
  5. Is ad spend separate from the management fee?
  6. Who do I contact, and how fast do they reply?
  7. What happens to my site and listings if I leave?
