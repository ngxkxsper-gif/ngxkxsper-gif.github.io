Privacy and message terms.

This covers the quote form on this site, replies you opt into, and package checkout links. It is a plain-language note for customers, not a lawyer’s contract.

Who we are

NDW INC, also called CMC — Creating Multiple Creations, builds websites for local service businesses. Contact winning@wealthyward.site or (615) 200-6415.

What the quote form collects

Name, business name, email, phone if you type one, trade, current website if you type one, what you need, a budget range, the package button you came from if any, and whether you checked the email option, the text option, or neither.

We use that to prepare a quote. We do not sell this information.

Email replies

The email checkbox is separate from the text checkbox and starts unchecked. Check it if NDW INC may email you a reply about this request. Leave it unchecked and we will not treat the form as permission to email you. You can ask us to stop emailing you by writing to winning@wealthyward.site.

Text messages

The text checkbox starts unchecked. Check it only if you agree to receive text messages from NDW INC about this request.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out of texts.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email winning@wealthyward.site.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase. You can request a quote, buy a package, or both, without agreeing to texts.

Stopping texts does not cancel a project you have already agreed to in writing.

Paying for a package

A Pay button opens a checkout page NDW INC has configured, such as a payment link from a card processor. This website does not collect or store card numbers. The processor’s own terms cover the payment. If a Pay link is not set up yet, the button says Get started and opens the quote form instead.

How the form is delivered

The site is static. Your browser sends the form to the form service named in our site configuration. That service delivers it to the studio inbox. Until that service is connected, the form tells you to email us directly and does not pretend the request was sent.
